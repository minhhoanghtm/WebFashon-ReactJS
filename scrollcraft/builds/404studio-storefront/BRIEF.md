# 404Studio storefront refresh

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Direction

1. Vibe: premium, minimalist, editorial, tactile, contemporary Vietnamese tailoring.
2. Journey: enter an atelier scene, discover the brand attitude, browse collections, choose a design, finish with the brand community.
3. Energy: quiet entry, growing curiosity through collections, strongest visual pause at the product edit, then a calm close.
4. Feeling curve: assurance in the hero, intrigue in the collection rail, confidence at the product edit, warmth in the close. Peak: "The products feel like an edited collection, not a crowded catalogue."
5. Signature move: the hero's photographic planes drift apart at different scroll rates while the copy holds its reading position.
6. Range: premium-minimal editorial.
7. Structure: distinct scenes, not a continuous worldflight.
8. Assets: preserve the supplied 404Studio imagery and product photography; do not generate replacements.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the fashion site where the atelier image opens up as you enter the collection."
